Common Questions

Why are my steps or activity not updating?

Open Stride and confirm Apple Health permissions are enabled. Activity data depends on Apple Health, device sensors, and background sync timing.

Why is my challenge score different from Apple Health?

Challenge data may update after Stride syncs with Apple Health and the backend. Open the app to force a fresh sync.

How do padel activities work?

You can start a padel session from iPhone or Apple Watch, track the score, then save the match summary with optional player names.

Can I use Stride without Apple Watch?

Yes. Core iPhone features work without Apple Watch. Apple Watch improves padel score tracking and some activity data quality.

Why did a medal not update?

Medals update from synced activity data. Some medals track current periods such as today or this week, while others track best-ever progress.

How do I delete my account?

Use the delete account option in Stride settings, or email support if you need help.
