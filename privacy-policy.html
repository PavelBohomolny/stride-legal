Privacy Policy

Stride helps you track activity, trainings, medals, challenges, friends, and Apple Watch padel sessions. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as your name, username, avatar, profile color, bio, and optional social usernames.
  • Activity data you choose to allow from Apple Health, such as steps, walking/running distance, active energy, exercise minutes, flights climbed, walking workouts, and routes for walk maps.
  • Training history you save in Stride, including sport type, date, duration, padel score, sets, and optional player names.
  • Challenge, leaderboard, medal, friend, invite, and notification data needed to run Stride social features.
  • Device and app data used for push notifications, widgets, crash reporting, analytics, and app reliability.

How We Use Information

  • To show your dashboard, activity rings, widgets, medals, history, and challenge progress.
  • To sync your activity and saved trainings across sessions and devices.
  • To support friends, leaderboards, challenge invites, and push notifications.
  • To improve app reliability with crash reports and basic analytics.
  • To protect the app from abuse and diagnose support issues.

Apple Health

Stride reads Health data only after you grant permission. You can change Health permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app. Stride does not sell Health data and does not use Health data for advertising.

Location

Stride may request location access to support background activity syncing and walk route maps. Location is used for these app features and is not sold.

Third-Party Services

Stride uses Supabase for backend storage and authentication-related data, Firebase for Analytics, Crashlytics, Messaging, and Google Sign-In, and Apple services for Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, notifications, widgets, and App Store review prompts.

Sharing

Some profile and challenge data may be visible to other users depending on your privacy settings and challenge participation. Stride does not post to social networks for you.

Data Retention

We keep account and activity data while your account is active or as needed to provide Stride. You may request deletion of your account and associated data from the app or by contacting support.

Your Choices

  • You can update profile details in the app.
  • You can control Health, location, and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete notifications and saved activities where supported in the app.
  • You can request account deletion by contacting support.

Children

Stride is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can delete it.
